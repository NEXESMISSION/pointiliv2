The one fact this plan is built on

A café that serves 150 coffees a day, with the live QR on the counter and a waiter who says «سكاني وخوذ تامبون», produces 20 to 40 stamps a day inside two weeks. Four real cafés together produced six in thirteen days. The screen is not on the counter. Until it is, nothing in sections 5 to 9 matters, and no owner will pay in October for something he has not seen work.

What already works

  • The product: a stamp lands in three seconds, no app, Tunisian by default, the QR cannot be photographed, the tests pass.
  • The price: 120 TND a year is a third of the nearest Tunisian competitor and needs no device.
  • The ask: the owner messages, you set everything up. No self-serve funnel to babysit.
  • The content: 33 posts in the owner's own words, an economics page, a reel kit, a caption maker.

What is missing

  • A routine that puts you in front of ten shop owners a morning.
  • A first paying shop, and a first photo of a real reward being handed over.
  • A reason for a shop to keep the screen on: a device, a poster, a staff sentence, a follow-up visit.
  • A presence where owners actually look you up: Facebook, WhatsApp Business, Google. The landing page's «كلّمنا اليوم» still sends them to a sign-up form.

2 · What we sell, to whom

Cafés first, one neighbourhood at a time

The promise in one sentence, the one the posts already use: الحريف اللي يرجع هو اللي يعيّش المحل. A reason to come back, on the customer's own phone, in three seconds, for ten dinars a month.

Why cafés, and why now

  • Frequency. A regular comes every day. A loyalty card that fills in ten days is felt; one that fills in a year is forgotten. Nothing else on the high street has a daily ticket.
  • The maths is small and true. One regular at 1.5 TND, 26 days a month, is 39 TND a month and about 470 TND a year. The reward, a free coffee, costs the owner half a dinar. The owner can check it in his head; post c14 already says it.
  • There are about 20,000 of them. The cafetiers' chamber counted roughly twenty thousand cafés in 2024, other estimates run higher. You do not need the market to be big. You need one zone to be dense.
  • The owner is at the counter. He decides on the spot, pays cash, and sees the stamps happen in front of him.

Then, and not yet

  • Next: barbers (one chair, one owner, a visit every two or three weeks at 8 to 15 TND), bakeries (daily), juice and fast-food. Same script, same zone, once the café script closes one in five.
  • Later: salons and gyms. Lower frequency, bigger ticket, more staff between the owner and the counter.
  • Not now: restaurants (weekly at best, waiter-mediated, owner rarely at the counter) and chains (they need staff accounts and branches, a second tier you have not built and should not build for the first 30 shops).

3 · Three problems, in order

Activation, then money, then a routine

  1. Activation, now until 11 October. The shops that said yes do not use it. The likely reasons, in the order I would check them: no device on the counter; the staff never got a sentence to say; the owner installed it, closed the tab and forgot; the reward chosen is not one a customer would chase; nobody came back after install day. The fix is not a message. It is a visit, with a phone stand and a laminated poster in your bag, where you put the screen on the counter with the owner and stamp his next ten customers yourself.
  2. First revenue, 11 to 20 October. Four trials end. Four conversations about money, at the counter, in cash. A shop with fewer than twenty stamps by then will not pay, and it should not: ask what stopped it and write the answer down. That answer is worth more than the 80 dinars. Target: two of the four pay.
  3. A repeatable way to win the next shop, from 12 October. Three mornings a week in one zone, ten doors a morning, every visit logged. Ten paying shops by 15 November, twenty-five by the end of December. Instagram is not this routine; it is what the owner checks that evening to see whether you are real.

4 · The offer

The price stays. The free month goes.

  • 120 TND a year, 80 TND for six months. Do not discount. Ten dinars a month is already «five coffees», and the cheapest thing in the market by a wide margin. A discount tells the owner the price was never real.
  • Paid on install day, in cash or D17. The four trial shops are what «try it and pay later» produces: nothing on the counter. From now on the standard first sale is the six-month plan, 80 TND, handed over before you leave. A shop that paid 80 dinars puts the tablet on the counter. The 30-day trial stays in the code as a safety so a shop can be live before a transfer clears, and the admin confirms the payment the same day. It is never said out loud, never posted, never offered.
  • «We set up everything» means this, in thirty minutes: the account, the three-question card, the logo, the counter poster printed and laminated, a phone stand, the shop's device open on the QR screen and plugged in, Pointili on the owner's own phone, the first five stamps with real customers while he watches, the staff sentence, and your WhatsApp number on the poster.
  • The renewal is the business. At six months the 80-dinar shop renews to the yearly plan at 120. The economics page already showed year one barely covers a visit; every dinar of margin is in the second year. Section 6 is about earning that renewal from day three.
The objection you will hear most

«ما عنديش تابلات» — the counter needs a screen showing the live code. Three answers, in the order to use them. One: any old Android phone plugged into a charger; most cafés have one in a drawer, and a used one costs about 100 dinars in any bazaar. Two: a Pointili «pack counter»: a used Android phone, a stand and a charger, sold at cost plus thirty dinars, or lent against a deposit. Three: if this is the first objection at ten of the first thirty visits, build the owner-confirms mode (printed code → the stamp request appears on the owner's phone → he taps ✓). Do not build it before you have heard it ten times.

Later, not now

  • A 50 TND setup fee the day a paid rep does the visit instead of you. Your own visit costs nothing; a rep's costs 35 to 60 dinars, and the fee pays for it the same day.
  • A second tier at 300 to 360 TND (branches, staff accounts, messaging customers, export) the day the third shop asks for any of those. Keep 120 as the door.

Referral, from the first paying shop

  • 20 TND cash to an owner for every shop he brings that pays. It is not a free offer; it is paying him for a visit you did not make, at a quarter of the first payment.
  • Ask at the moment a reward is first handed over, when he is pleased: «شكون تعرف من أصحاب المحلات هوني؟»

5 · Winning a shop

One zone, ten doors a morning, every visit written down

Pick one zone and stay in it until it is done: the neighbourhood of the four trial shops or your own, a walk of about three kilometres with a hundred or more cafés. Density is the strategy, not a convenience. An owner sees the card in the phone of a customer who got it next door. Owners talk to each other across the street. Ten visits fit in one morning on foot. A support visit on day three costs you twenty minutes, not a taxi.

10 doors a morning × 3 mornings a week = 120 visits a month owner present about half the time → 60 conversations listens to the two-minute demo about six in ten → 36 demos closes, on the spot or on the Thursday return, one in four or five → 7 to 11 shops a month, alone If after 60 visits fewer than one in twenty closes, the effort is not the problem. Change one thing, the opening line, the price framing, the zone, and measure again.

In the bag

  • A second phone or tablet with the demo café's QR screen open, charged.
  • Five phone stands (a bazaar, 10 to 15 TND each).
  • A roll of stickers: سكاني وخوذ تامبون, for the counter and the till.
  • Cards with the WhatsApp number and the Instagram QR.
  • A receipt book. Cash needs a paper receipt with the shop's name, the plan and the date.
  • Your own D17 QR printed, for owners who do not carry cash.
  • The counter poster is per shop (it carries the shop's join code), so it comes on the day-three visit, laminated. Or print it at the nearest librairie the same morning.

The visit log

One line per door, every door, including the ones that said no: shop, owner's first name, phone, outcome, the exact objection, the date to return. In a notebook or a sheet, it does not matter. This list is the business. In three months it tells you the close rate, the objection to answer in the next post, and which street to walk again.

6 · Keeping a shop

Activated in fourteen days, renewed on the strength of its own numbers

A shop is activated when it has 20 stamps and 10 customers within fourteen days of install. Below that, it is a shop you have not finished selling. The economics page said the whole business lives in the renewal; the renewal is decided in the first two weeks, when the screen either stays on the counter or goes back in the drawer.

7 · Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Google

Online is where the owner checks whether you are real

Content is not the acquisition channel for the first hundred shops. It is the credibility check. The owner you visited at nine will look you up at nine in the evening. What he must find: a page that posts three times a week, real shops, real stamps, a WhatsApp button that answers.

Instagram

  • Publish the thirty finished posts, Monday, Wednesday and Friday evenings, in the order PLAN.md already set: what it is, a day at the counter, «does the customer need an app», the reward examples, cheating, paper cards, «is it hard», the price, the FAQ. Then the second wave.
  • Stop producing new carousels until ten shops pay. After that the content is the shops: the first reward at robusta, the poster on a real counter, an owner saying one sentence to camera. One of those is worth ten carousels.
  • Keep the reel kit for exactly that: a nine-second phone clip of a real scan, captions from the caption maker.

Facebook, the same day

  • A Pointili page, the same name and cover, the same posts the same evening. The café owner over thirty-five lives on Facebook, not Instagram. This costs nothing and doubles the reach of everything already made.
  • Post the reward examples (c8, c30, c54) in the groups where café owners buy equipment and hire staff. No selling in the text, just the question and the picture.

WhatsApp Business

  • The support number on a WhatsApp Business profile: a greeting in Tunisian, the two plans in the catalogue, quick replies for the five FAQ.
  • The landing page's «كلّمنا اليوم» and «ابدا توّا» currently open the sign-up form. Point them at WhatsApp with a prefilled message, like the billing page already does. The whole positioning is «message us, we set it up»; the site should do what the posts say.

Google, and the customers themselves

  • A Google Business Profile for Pointili so «carte fidélité Tunisie» finds a real business with a phone number. The site's SEO is already done.
  • Every stamp puts Pointili in a customer's phone; the customer home already links your Instagram. Add one button, «اقترح محلّك»: the customer names a shop he would like to see it in, you get a lead and an introduction.
  • Paid ads only after ten paying shops and two owners on camera, then 100 TND on the c40 to c43 ads, Tunis, small business owners, WhatsApp as the button, judged in conversations not likes. Meta needs a foreign-currency card; sort that out before you need it.

What the table means

At the base speed Pointili pays one person a modest salary by this time next year and nothing more. It becomes a company at five hundred shops, or sooner with a second tier and customer messaging, and that is a 2027–2028 question. The 2026 questions are only two: does one visit in five close, and do the first six-month shops renew in April. Everything else is noise until those two numbers exist.

When to add a person

  • Not a salary before thirty paying shops. A rep at 1,600 TND a month has to close fourteen shops a month just to cover himself at 120 each.
  • Commission first: 30 TND per paying shop to one person who already walks those streets, a delivery rider, a student, a cousin with a moto. Give him the demo phone and the script; keep the install visit for yourself.
  • The setup fee from section 4 arrives with the first salaried rep, not before.

The levers, in order of weight

  • Renewal rate. 50 per cent makes this a job; 80 per cent makes each shop worth 600 TND over its life.
  • Close rate per visit. Decides whether one person can reach a hundred shops in a year.
  • A second tier. The only way revenue per shop moves, and it needs shops asking first.
  • Price. The weakest lever. Leave it.

10 · Product work that serves growth

Seven small things, and a list of what not to build

  1. Landing buttons to WhatsApp. «كلّمنا اليوم» opens a prefilled WhatsApp message; the sign-up form stays reachable for the rare owner who insists. An hour.
  2. Vercel Pro the day the first shop pays. The Hobby plan is for non-commercial use; 63 TND a month keeps the deployment inside the terms it runs under.
  3. «اقترح محلّك» on the customer home. A shop name and a neighbourhood, stored with the customer who suggested it. Half a day, and it turns every stamp collector into a scout.
  4. The counter-screen poll, before the tenth paying shop. 900 ms for twenty seconds after a stamp, four seconds while nothing happens. The only cost that grows with every shop, divided by four, and nobody at a counter notices.
  5. The renewal message, before February. Day 150 for six-month plans, day 335 for yearly, carrying the shop's own numbers and a D17 link. The first six-month renewals fall in March and April 2027.
  6. Password reset by hand until thirty shops. The SMS code is generated and never sent because no gateway is configured. The admin resets it on a phone call today; wire a gateway when the calls become weekly.
  7. Owner-confirms mode, only if the field asks for it. Printed code, request on the owner's phone, one tap. Ten objections first.
Not before thirty paying shops

Branches and staff accounts, customer messaging, a second tier, more carousels, French marketing, a card processor, new trade categories on the site, a redesign of anything. Every one of these is a reason not to be on a street at nine in the morning.
