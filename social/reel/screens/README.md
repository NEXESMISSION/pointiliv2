# Reel screens

Real app screens on clean phone frames, transparent around the phone, 1210 × 2713 px (3×), ready to drop into a 1080 × 1920 reel. Made from the demo shop (Café Bonheur) and a throwaway customer by `node --env-file=.env.local scripts/reel-screens.mjs` on the local dev server.

| client-2-first-stamp | Stamp 1/10 just landed, with the card | التامبون يتسجّل في ثانية |
