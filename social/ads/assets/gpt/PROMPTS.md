# Pictures for the ads — to make with ChatGPT (images)

Save each one here, under the name given, as a .jpg (portrait 9:16, 1080×1920 or larger).
`node render.mjs f4` then films the picture in place of the drawn scene — nothing else to change.

Always the same instruction at the end of each prompt, so they look like one series:

> Vertical 9:16 photo, natural light, shot on a phone, shallow depth of field, warm and real,
> Tunisian café (Tunis / Sfax), no text, no logos, no brand names, no watermark.
