# Pointili — the video ads: what is said

Each film is said in Derja, a line at a time — the lines below are the ones the voices say now (a person reading, one take a film). To give a film another voice: record its lines **in one take**, on any phone (a quiet room, the phone 20 cm away), then

```
node take.mjs f1 "C:/path/to/take.wav"
node retime.mjs f1
node render.mjs f1
```

The take is heard on this computer and kept whole; the film re-times to it: scenes and subtitles follow the real voice. Say a line another way than it is written and the film's line must change with it (edit it in films/, then `node scripts.mjs`): the subtitle shows what is heard.

Nothing in these films promises a notification: the customer sees the card on his phone, the owner writes to him himself, on WhatsApp.
