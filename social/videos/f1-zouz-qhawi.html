f1 — «زوز قهاوي» (two cafés on the same street): same coffee, same price; one is empty, the other's customers keep coming back. Why? The second one did not leave the next visit to luck: the code on the counter, the card in the customer's phone, the free coffee getting closer, «يبعدو» and a gift tampon on WhatsApp when he drifts. «It has a system.
