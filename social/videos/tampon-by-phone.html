Made by

Filmed
Live screen recording (Playwright, 432x768, scaled to 720x1280) of the real app on a local dev server: a robot owner «Bader» with the perfume shop «Sultan al oud», a robot customer «أحمد» with 2 tampons already. The step line is a dark rounded box (white bold text, purple number badge) injected into the page, at the bottom (step 2 at the top). The finger is an injected white dot with an orange ring that slides to each target and shrinks on the press; the clicks are real.
Times
approximate (±0.2 s): a live recording with fixed waits (600, 1800, 800+180 per tap, 900, 1500, 700, 1200, 8 digits × 170, 1800, 1500, 900, 1500, 3500 ms) plus page loads, trimmed 0.6 s at the start. Times below are anchored on visible changes measured in the file with ffprobe scene scores (0.68 caption 1, 4.20 camera opens, 5.08 caption 2, 8.40 typing screen, 11.3–12.7 digits, 13.44 customer found, 14.72 caption 4, 17.64 tampon given, 19.84 last caption). Each caption stays until the next replaces it. Step 5 «انزل على «زيد تامبون»» is in the script but never appears: in this app version tapping the perfume gives the tampon itself, so the script finds no «زيد تامبون» button and skips that step — the sequence jumps from 4 to ✓.
