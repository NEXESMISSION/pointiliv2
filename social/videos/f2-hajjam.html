Script

f2 — «الحريف اللي غاب» (the customer who stopped coming): a barber's regular disappears, his paper card was lost on the first day; with Pointili the card is in his phone, the owner sees him drifting away and sends him a gift tampon on WhatsApp. Flat motion graphics, every piece drawn here. Modelled on the structure of a barber-shop reel (hook → loss → card in the phone → bring him back → «send this to a friend who has a salon»), in our words and only what Pointili really does: no notification is promised — the owner writes, by hand, on WhatsApp.
